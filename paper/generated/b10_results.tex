\textbf{Results.} The author labelled 17 atomic obligations across the ten texts. He drafted them first and then entered them in the labelling tool; the entries span 3 minutes, which is entry time, not total labelling effort. Table~\ref{tab:b10} gives per-arm results. The guided minus naive difference in macro F1, with 95\% bootstrap intervals over texts, was Qwen2.5-1.5B: -0.010 [-0.121, +0.090], guided better on 3 of 10 texts; Mistral-7B: +0.050 [+0.000, +0.117], guided better on 2 of 10 texts.
