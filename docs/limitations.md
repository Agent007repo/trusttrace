# Limitations

We have no practitioner data and no measured elicitation advantage. The initial requirements are authored synthetic concerns, not regulatory obligations. The catalogue and decisions are incomplete relative to the release brief. Citation membership does not prove entailment. Confidence is not calibrated. The field allowlist does not remove sensitive information inside an allowed free-text field.

The mock UI is intentionally local. It has no identity provider, durable database, persistent audit anchor or production threat model. The hash chain detects tampering only relative to a trusted supplied anchor. Rewriting both chain and anchor defeats it.

Regulatory mappings are illustrative design work, not legal advice. No AI Act high-risk classification is asserted. Legal excerpt reuse and article-level applicability remain blocked until the operative texts are retrieved and reviewed.
