# ADR-004: Verify hash chains against a separate anchor

Status: accepted for the foundation only. This is an architecture decision record (ADR).

## Context and decision drivers

A hash chain alone cannot detect a wholesale rewrite or suffix deletion. Requirements: TR-012, TR-013, TR-014.

## Considered options

Plain events; hash chain alone; chain plus separately retained count and head; external signing service.

## Decision outcome

Chain plus a separately retained count and head. External storage is pending.

## Consequences

The prototype detects mutation against the supplied anchor. An attacker controlling both chain and anchor defeats it. No durable or append-only storage is claimed.

## Reflection

An operator may dislike external retention. Availability bias could favour a familiar hash-chain design. Compare signed checkpoints and protected storage before release.

The reflection subsection is our proposed adaptation, motivated by the Ethics-Aware DecidArch abstract linked in the source register. We have not validated this adaptation with architects.
