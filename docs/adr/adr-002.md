# ADR-002: Require review for every compliance suggestion

Status: accepted for the foundation only. This is an architecture decision record (ADR).

## Context and decision drivers

An uncalibrated confidence signal cannot authorise a customs decision. Requirements: TR-003, TR-004, TR-005, TR-006, TR-007, TR-008, TR-016.

## Considered options

Auto-approve above threshold; review only low scores; review all answers.

## Decision outcome

Review all answers. Low scores explain urgency; they never grant authority.

## Consequences

We add review effort and cannot measure its impact without practitioners. State is currently in memory.

## Reflection

A high-throughput broker may object to every-item review. Automation bias is plausible. Compare reviewer effort and error recovery in the future study.

The reflection subsection is our proposed adaptation, motivated by the Ethics-Aware DecidArch abstract linked in the source register. We have not validated this adaptation with architects.
