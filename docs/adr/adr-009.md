# ADR-009: Make contests and overrides first-class audited records

Status: accepted for the synthetic reference implementation. This is an architecture decision record (ADR).

## Context and decision drivers

Mandatory human review (ADR-002) handles the reviewer. It gives the affected party, such as a consignee, no route to challenge a suggestion. A reviewer who changes a suggestion should not erase what the model proposed.

Requirements: TR-029, TR-037. Conflict: CONFLICT-05.

## Considered options

Free-text comments; reject-and-retype; explicit contest records and override records that keep the original suggestion and a required rationale.

## Decision outcome

Contests are records with a party, a reason and a resolution by a named reviewer. Overrides keep the original suggestion object, the replacement and a non-empty rationale. Both are written to the audit chain.

## Consequences

More records slow throughput and add reviewer work. The prototype has no notification or deadline for resolving contests.

## Reflection

Assumption: affected parties can be identified and reached. A broker may see contest volume as a cost centre. Possible bias: treating contestability as a feature rather than a process with staffing costs. Check: we recorded resolution identity so unresolved contests remain visible.

The reflection subsection is our proposed adaptation, motivated by the Ethics-Aware DecidArch abstract linked in the source register. We have not validated this adaptation with architects.
