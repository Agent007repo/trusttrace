# ADR-013: Separate quoted source text from generated interpretation

Status: accepted for the synthetic reference implementation. This is an architecture decision record (ADR).

## Context and decision drivers

Reviewers over-trust fluent answers. A citation that resolves to a retrieved passage proves membership, not that the passage supports the claim.

Requirements: TR-025, TR-030, TR-035.

## Considered options

Inline answer with footnotes; answer plus raw passages; a card that shows verbatim quotes with version and location, the generated text under a distinct label, and an explicit citation-check statement.

## Decision outcome

Explanation cards list each cited passage with its source version and locator, show the generated interpretation separately with a model-output label, and state that only membership was checked.

## Consequences

Cards are longer and may be skimmed. Version and locator are only as good as the source register, which is synthetic here.

## Reflection

Assumption: reviewers read labels. A broker under time pressure may ignore them. Possible bias: designing for an idealised careful reviewer. Check: we flagged reviewer behaviour as a question for the practitioner study rather than claiming the card prevents over-trust.

The reflection subsection is our proposed adaptation, motivated by the Ethics-Aware DecidArch abstract linked in the source register. We have not validated this adaptation with architects.
