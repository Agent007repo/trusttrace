# ADR-005: Begin with deterministic consistency checks

Status: accepted for the foundation only. This is an architecture decision record (ADR).

## Context and decision drivers

Packages, shipment ID and weight offer concrete initial fit criteria. Requirements: TR-015.

## Considered options

Delegate all checking to an LLM; deterministic equality checks; normalised semantic comparisons.

## Decision outcome

Deterministic checks over three fields, flag missing fields.

## Consequences

Units, locale-specific numbers and equivalent formatted values need later normalisation. These are authored examples, not a calibrated document parser.

## Reflection

A broker will expect field normalisation. Our own examples may hide heterogeneity. Collect counterexamples before changing comparison semantics.

The reflection subsection is our proposed adaptation, motivated by the Ethics-Aware DecidArch abstract linked in the source register. We have not validated this adaptation with architects.
