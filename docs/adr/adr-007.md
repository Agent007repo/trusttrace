# ADR-007: Version the sharing policy and give owners disclosure receipts

Status: accepted for the synthetic reference implementation. This is an architecture decision record (ADR).

## Context and decision drivers

ADR-001 introduced a field allowlist. Two gaps remained: an allowlist that changes in place silently rewrites what earlier decisions meant, and data owners cannot see what left on their behalf.

Requirements: TR-021, TR-022, TR-039, TR-040. Conflict: CONFLICT-03.

## Considered options

Mutable allowlist; append-only policy versions; policy versions plus per-disclosure receipts.

## Decision outcome

Every policy change produces a new numbered version. Each share returns a receipt listing shared fields, withheld fields, the policy version and a payload hash. A contract helper rejects any field outside the approved table. Consignee documents are keyed by consignee so one consignee cannot list or read another's.

## Consequences

Receipts are themselves data that must be stored and protected. Versioning grows the policy history. Receipts prove what the policy released, not what a recipient did afterwards.

## Reflection

Assumption: owners want receipts and will read them. A carrier might object that receipts expose its access patterns to the shipper. Possible bias: confirmation bias toward transparency because it is easy to test. Check: we kept receipts free of field values, only names and a hash.

The reflection subsection is our proposed adaptation, motivated by the Ethics-Aware DecidArch abstract linked in the source register. We have not validated this adaptation with architects.
