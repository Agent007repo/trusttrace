# ADR-008: Purge content at retention expiry, keep a hash and lifecycle metadata

Status: accepted for the synthetic reference implementation. This is an architecture decision record (ADR).

## Context and decision drivers

Audit needs durable evidence; data minimisation needs documents to disappear when no longer needed. Keeping everything satisfies the auditor and fails the data protection officer, and deleting everything does the reverse.

Requirements: TR-028. Conflict: CONFLICT-04.

## Considered options

Keep documents indefinitely; delete documents and their audit references; delete content but keep a content hash, expiry and purge time.

## Decision outcome

Delete document content at expiry and keep only a content hash and lifecycle metadata. The hash still lets an auditor confirm that a later-produced copy matches what was processed.

## Consequences

Which metadata is lawful to keep, and for how long, is a legal question we have not answered. A hash of low-entropy data can be brute-forced, so the hash is not anonymisation.

## Reflection

Assumption: a hash is acceptable minimal evidence. An auditor may want the content itself. Possible bias: availability of a neat technical compromise. Check: the test asserts that no field value survives in the metadata.

The reflection subsection is our proposed adaptation, motivated by the Ethics-Aware DecidArch abstract linked in the source register. We have not validated this adaptation with architects.
