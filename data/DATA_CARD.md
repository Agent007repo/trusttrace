# Data card: synthetic foundation case

All case concerns and shipment examples were authored with an AI coding assistant from Samarth's brief. They are synthetic design inputs. No practitioner interviews, real company documents, human gold annotations or operative legal excerpts are included. Public Commission summaries inform the general case framing but do not establish binding article mappings.

The initial catalogue has a bounded tested slice and unimplemented future concerns. It is not the final 40 to 60 requirement catalogue. The default roles and trust relationships simplify a multi-party ecosystem and require later expansion. The licence of original authored case text is CC BY 4.0.

Annotation inputs remain separate from the requirements catalogue. Do not copy generated catalogue content into a human gold file.
